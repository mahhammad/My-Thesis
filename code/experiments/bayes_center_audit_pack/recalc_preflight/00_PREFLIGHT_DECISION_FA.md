## 3. Numerical proof policy and fail-closed stops
Let `Lhat,Uhat` be returned float64 bounds for target `T` and let `eps_L, eps_U, eps_T` be **demonstrated** nonnegative absolute error bounds (kernel construction, denominator, accumulation, alpha, target serialization/semantics) from an *independent* precision methodology. Report `gap_above=T-Uhat` and `gap_below=Lhat-T` and `eps_above=eps_U+eps_T`, `eps_below=eps_L+eps_T`. Only if `gap_above>eps_above` or `gap_below>eps_below` may that coordinate be proposed as `UNATTAINABLE_BY_VALID_OUTER_BOUND`. When `eps` is unavailable, insufficient, or gap overlaps its envelope: **`INCONCLUSIVE`**. Within all necessary coordinate intervals is always **`INCONCLUSIVE`**, never `ATTAINABLE`. `1e-10` from toy tests is an assertion tolerance, **not** an interval arithmetic proof or generally valid epsilon.

Two alternative precision gates must be evaluated *before any scientific certificate*:
1. **High-precision or directed-rounding interval arithmetic:** independently recompute elementary Gaussian weights, masked origin-excluded normalizations, first-hop, DP min/max and class means with outward enclosures, including representational uncertainty; issue interval-enclosed lower/upper when widths are small relative to gap. No float64-only "universal certified" label.
2. **Distinct implementation cross-check:** construct from raw pairwise Gaussian distances, independently normalize legal destinations and origin-removal, use different summation/traversal algorithm; compare 3×3, per-origin extrema and H2 root. Cross-check alone is an empirical error diagnostic, not an a priori error proof. Narrow gaps require option (1) or conservative `INCONCLUSIVE`.

**Hard stop and append-only FAIL** on any hash/code drift, data identity gap, target mismatch, invalid/missing feature, nonfinite `B/den/alpha/value/output`, `den<=1e-12` for any *reachable and selectable* feature, illegal action or zero legal actions, `lower>upper`, range beyond [0,1], discrepancy exceeding prespecified independent tolerance, thread budget breach, or CPU/wall/RSS limit. V2 guards use 1e-10 for output-shape validation; the supervisor should reject any inconsistency, **not clip**. For states unreachable under the fixed initial-origin walk, fail-closed policy must be consistent with the V2 code's stronger no-dead-end assumption; this is a documented domain restriction, not silent coercion. Never partially commit outputs as PASS after failure. A missing, late, or incomplete progress log is itself failure.
